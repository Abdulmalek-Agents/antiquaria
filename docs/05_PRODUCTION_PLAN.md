# 📅 Production Plan — Antiquaria

> 10-month ship plan. Phases are ordered; specific durations are realistic but flex with team availability.

## Team

- 1× Lead designer/programmer (full-time)
- 1× Illustrator (part-time, 4 months commissioned)
- 1× Composer (commissioned, 2 months total)
- 1× Writer (part-time, full project)
- Optional: 1× QA tester (last 6 weeks)

Total payroll + commissions estimate: **$25–40k**.

## Phase plan (10 months)

### Phase 1 — Foundation
*Output: pinboard demo + 3 cases playable*

- Set up Unity 6 LTS 2D-URP project
- Implement ServiceLocator, SaveService, EventBus
- Implement PinboardService + drag-drop + red-string
- Implement MagnifierController + HotspotSO reveal
- Implement DialogueService wrapper for Pixel Crushers
- Author 3 placeholder cases (Halloway watch + Dunne locket + 1 referral)
- Internal milestone: 3-case playable build

### Phase 2 — Act 1 vertical slice
*Output: Steam Next Fest-ready demo (45 min)*

- Commission illustrator: shop interior + 4 portraits + ~20 objects
- Commission composer: 2 tracks (theme + Act 1 ambient)
- Author 6 Act 1 cases
- Implement HintService
- Implement ReputationService
- Polish: page-flip shader, audio mixing, accessibility presets
- Internal milestone: Act 1 full playthrough

### Phase 3 — Acts 2–3 content
*Output: ~5h playable build*

- Commission illustrator: 8 more portraits + 30 objects + Act 2/3 environments
- Compose: 2 more tracks
- Author Acts 2 & 3 (12 cases)
- Implement UV lamp tool (Act 2)
- Implement multi-customer concurrent flow (Act 3)

### Phase 4 — Steam Next Fest + wishlist push
*Output: Public demo, 7,000 wishlists target*

- Publish Steam page
- Submit to Steam Next Fest
- Coordinate creator outreach (ManlyBadassHero, CJUGames, Smii7y, Strange Horticulture community)
- Marketing GIF library produced
- TikTok shorts published (1/week)
- Internal milestone: 7,000 wishlists

### Phase 5 — Acts 4–6 content
*Output: complete game build*

- Commission illustrator: remaining 10 portraits + 30 objects + 3 environments
- Compose: final 1 track (finale + credits)
- Author Acts 4, 5, 6 (16 cases)
- Implement Black Auction moral choice mechanic
- Implement Marguerite-mystery cross-Act chain
- Optional: commission VO for top 6 customers

### Phase 6 — Polish + accessibility certification
*Output: cert-ready build*

- Full accessibility pass (Priya's spec)
- Localisation: confirm EN; queue ES/DE/FR/JP for post-launch
- Steam achievements (~20)
- Steam Cloud save
- Steam Deck verified submission
- Closed beta with 50 players from Strange Horticulture community

### Phase 7 — Launch
*Output: Day 1 release*

- Steam Day 1 1.0 launch
- Live community moderation (Amara)
- Patch hotfixes
- Press/creator embargo coordination

### Phase 8 — Post-launch (3 months tail)
*Output: 2 free updates + paid DLC case pack*

- Free Update 1: 4 new cases + 2 new customers
- Free Update 2: Switch parity prep
- Paid DLC 1: "The Dublin Branch" ($4.99, 10 cases)

## Risk register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Illustrator scope creep | Lock 80 objects + 22 portraits + 6 environments in contract |
| Composer delays | Pre-buy royalty-free fallback OST for placeholder |
| Strange Horticulture II surprise announcement | Monitor monthly; can shift launch ±60 days |
| Late accessibility blockers | Embed Priya's checklist into Phase 2 (not last-mile) |
| Wishlist below 5,000 at Phase 4 end | Marketing escalation: paid X/Bluesky ads + publisher outreach (Iceberg, Annapurna scout list) |
