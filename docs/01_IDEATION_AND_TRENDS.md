### 2.3 Why this isn't oversaturated like cozy farming

The cozy genre has **two distinct audiences** that the market often conflates:
1. *Cozy farming/relationships* — saturated. Stardew, Coral Island, Fields of Mistria, Sun Haven, Mineko, Echoes of the Plum Grove, Witchbrook.
2. *Cozy puzzle/investigation* — undersupplied. Strange Horticulture is the ONLY major occupant.

Antiquaria targets audience #2 with zero direct competitor announced for 2027.
