# ⚠️ Antiquaria — Deprecation Notice

## Status
**Halted as of 2026-05-22.** No new development planned.

## Why

Antiquaria was conceived and approved as a low-budget, ~10-month, **2D** indie ship. The 14-expert board scored it 8.43/10 — the highest of 6 candidates — and committed to its production.

After founder review, the studio direction was clarified: **all Inventix titles will be 3D-native** to align with the studio's existing 3D portfolio (Ashen Veil, Hollow Quota, Hearth & Hex) and to leverage the team's growing 3D production capacity.

A forced 2D → 3D port for Antiquaria would:
- Sacrifice the tactile-pinboard mechanic that is the game's signature USP
- Lose the pen-and-ink + watercolor visual identity (the unique selling lever Elena identified)
- Drive the team's cost from ~$30k (2D shippable) to ~$300k (forced 3D port)
- Break the 1-year ship constraint without proportional commercial upside

The expanded 18-expert board (with 4 new 3D specialists) reviewed the situation and **unanimously voted to deprecate rather than port**. The 2D design is preserved here for historical reference.

## What replaces it

The board selected two 3D-native concepts as Antiquaria's portfolio replacements:

| Replacement | Genre | Repo |
|---|---|---|
| 🪡 Aetherloom | 3D 2-player co-op action roguelite (Hades × Children of Morta) | [aetherloom](https://github.com/Abdulmalek-Agents/aetherloom) |
| 🦋 Lumen Isles | 3D cozy photography exploration (Pokémon Snap × Eastshade) | [lumen-isles](https://github.com/Abdulmalek-Agents/lumen-isles) |
